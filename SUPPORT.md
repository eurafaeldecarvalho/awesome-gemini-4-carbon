# Support

This is a curated list of other people's public posts. It is not a Google product and it cannot grant access to Gemini 4 Carbon.

- New creation: open a Suggest an entry issue ([template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/suggest-entry.yml)), or a pull request that edits `data/entries.yml`.
- Broken link: open a Broken link issue ([template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/broken-link.yml)) with the URL and the entry id.
- Wrong credit, or you want your post removed: open an issue with the post URL.

Please don't send model-access requests or private prompts. If the prompt was not published by the author, it cannot be added.
